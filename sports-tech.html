When Tech
Kills the Thrill

Sport is emotion, chaos, the moment you don't know what happens next. What are we losing?

VAR Delay

Players half-celebrate, look to the screen, wait 2 minutes for a toe-offside check. The goal becomes a question mark.

MLB Shift Ban

Every team used data to run identical defensive shifts — so efficient, so boring. MLB banned it in 2023 to restore creativity.

NBA Analytics

Analytics proved the three-pointer reigns supreme. Now every team takes the same shots. The improvised fadeaway? Calculated away.

Data, by definition, tries to eliminate the unexpected. The magic of sport has always lived in the unexpected.

Finding the
Balance

The question is not whether we use technology. That ship has sailed.
The question is: are we using it as a tool — or letting it become the master?

Technology SHOULD

  • Sharpen officiating decisions
  • Keep athletes safer from injury
  • Bring sport to global audiences
  • Set boundaries on unfair advantages

Technology SHOULD NOT

  • Replace human instinct and creativity
  • Eliminate the unpredictable moments
  • Reduce players to spreadsheet executors
  • Define what counts as human achievement

We don't watch sport to see perfect execution. We watch because we don't know what happens next.
